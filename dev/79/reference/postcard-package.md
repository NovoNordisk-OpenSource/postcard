# postcard: Estimating Marginal Effects with Prognostic Covariate Adjustment

Conduct power analyses and inference of marginal effects. Uses plug-in
estimation and influence functions to perform robust inference,
optionally leveraging historical data to increase precision with
prognostic covariate adjustment. The methods are described in
Højbjerre-Frandsen et al. (2025)
[doi:10.48550/arXiv.2503.22284](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.22284)
.
